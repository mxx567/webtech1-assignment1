Gabe Newell

About Gabe Newell

Gabe Newell was born on November 3, 1962, and is still alive today creating incredible technologies. He is the co-founder and president of the legendary video game development and digital distribution company Valve Corporation. His most famous contribution to the world is Steam, which completely redefined how people buy and play PC games globally. Under his leadership, Valve also created timeless video game masterpieces like Half-Life, Portal, and Counter-Strike.

Co-founder of Valve & Creator of Steam

Important Events

  1. 1962: Born in Colorado, United States.
  2. 1983: Dropped out of Harvard University to work at Microsoft.
  3. 1996: Left Microsoft after 13 years to found Valve Corporation with Mike Harrington.
  4. 1998: Valve released Half-Life, which became one of the greatest games in history.
  5. 2003: Launched Steam, changing digital game distribution forever.

Key Contributions

  • Created the Steam platform for PC gaming.
  • Developed the Source game engine.
  • Funded and popularized modern VR headsets (HTC Vive and Valve Index).
  • Released the Steam Deck, a revolutionary handheld gaming computer.
